The Best Pancakes You'll Ever Make

Description

Method for making perfectly fluffy pancakes at home, every time. Never subject yourself to flat, lifeless pancakes again!

Ingredients

  • 21/4 Cups of milk
  • 1/4 Cup of vegetable oil
  • 1 Tbsp of vanilla extract
  • 1/4 Cup of apple cider vinegar
  • 3 Eggs
  • 3 Cups of all-purpose-flour
  • 1/4 Cup of sugar
  • 11/2 Tsp of salt
  • 1 Tbsp of baking powder
  • 11/2 Tsp of baking soda

Steps

  1. Fully mix all dry ingredients
  2. Fully mix all wet ingredients
  3. Mix dry and wet ingredients together stirring between 20 and 25 times (DO NOT over do it, lumps are okay!)
  4. Heat the pan of your preference using low heat
  5. Put about one tbsp of whipped salted butter or regular butter in the pan (adding as needed)
  6. Once melted get a generous amount of batter using the spoon of your preference (berry spoons are great!)
  7. Do not turn the pancakes too quickly, watch for the bubbles to pop up before you start to flip them
  8. Using the spatula of your preference flip the pancakes doing a quick push (avoid pressing them down!)
  9. You can move them around to get them to soak up some butter up
  10. If when you push them with your fingers they spring back they are done! (If you see batter when pushing them they are not done just yet)
  11. Enjoy! Use your favorite toppings with them. Mapple syrup, butter, strawberries, anything works!
